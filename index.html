How a tape comes together

Scroll along — this is the whole process, start to finish.

To begin

Write your affirmations, in as many layers as you like

Type freely into the box. Split your words across up to five layers — maybe one is gentle and slow, another quick and insistent.

Let it speak

Each layer is turned into spoken audio, tuned your way

Save a layer and it's voiced automatically. From there, set its speed — from a calm 1x to a whisper-fast 10x — and its own volume.

Build the world around it

Layer in ambiance, frequencies, tones, and music

Preview rain, solfeggio frequencies, isochronic tones, lo-fi, and more — add whatever supports your intention.

Check your work

See the whole mix laid out in one place

Every layer, every added sound, its speed and its volume — all summarized before you commit to anything.

Take it with you

Save your tape and download the finished file

One button renders everything into a single audio file, ready to keep and listen to whenever you like.
